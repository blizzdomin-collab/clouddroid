Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our consulting packages, payment terms, and engagement process.

What services does Clouddroid FinOps Consulting offer?

We offer three fixed-fee consulting packages: Starter Audit (£499) for infrastructure spend review and cost optimisation report; Professional Engagement (£999) for IT budget strategy, vendor negotiation support and 3-month follow-up; and Enterprise Programme (£2,499) for full FinOps implementation with governance framework, team training and 6-month support.

Who are these services intended for?

Our services are offered exclusively on a business-to-business basis. They are designed for UK and EU-based companies, typically SMEs and mid-market organisations with annual cloud spend above £50,000 who want an independent advisory engagement rather than a long-term managed service.

Are you a regulated financial advisor?

No. PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA), the Central Bank of Ireland, or any other UK or EU financial services regulator.

We are a UK private limited company providing FinOps and IT cost optimisation consulting. We do not provide and are not authorised to provide:

  • Investment advice or investment management services
  • Portfolio management or capital allocation advice
  • Insurance, mortgage, or credit broking
  • Banking, payment, or e-money services (payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., an authorised payment institution)
  • Tax, accounting, audit, or legal services

Our deliverables are informational and advisory only and do not constitute regulated financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice. Clients must seek advice from an appropriately qualified and independently regulated professional before making any financial, investment, or business decision based on our work.

How are payments processed?

All payments are processed securely by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., an authorised payment institution regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay. PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD does not store, process, or have access to your full card number or CVV.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all consulting packages purchased directly through this website. If you cancel within 14 days of purchase and no consulting services have been delivered during that period, you will receive a full refund. Once services have been delivered (for example a report has been issued or a workshop has been conducted), fees are non-refundable due to the bespoke nature of consulting work. See our Refund Policy for full details.

How long does each engagement take?

Trial Audit: deliverables within 5 business days. Starter Audit: deliverables within 10 business days. Professional Engagement: within 20 business days. Enterprise Programme: within 45 business days. Timeframes commence once we have received all required information from you.

What information do you need from us to start?

We typically need read-only or invoiced access to your cloud billing accounts (AWS, Azure, GCP or other), a list of current vendors, and a 60-minute kick-off call with your finance and engineering stakeholders. For Enterprise engagements we also request a current org chart and any existing FinOps documentation.

Are you VAT registered?

PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD is not currently registered for VAT. All fees shown are exclusive of VAT and no VAT is charged on invoices. If and when we register for VAT, clients will be notified in advance and VAT will be applied in accordance with UK HMRC rules.

How is my data protected?

We process your personal data in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. We have appointed a Data Protection Officer reachable at dpo@clouddroid.eu. We use Stripe for payment processing, which is PCI DSS Level 1 compliant. Card data never touches our servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details and your rights.

What laws govern these services?

All engagements are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
