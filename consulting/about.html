About Clouddroid

Clouddroid is the consulting brand of PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD, a UK private limited company providing independent FinOps advisory to ambitious technology organisations.

Our story

PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD was founded to provide business clients with clear, independent advisory on cloud financial management. We saw too many organisations paying for tooling, capacity and licences they did not need — not because their teams lacked skill, but because they did not have a second opinion from someone with both finance and engineering depth.

We are deliberately small, deliberately independent, and deliberately focused. We do not sell software, we do not take referral fees from cloud vendors, and we do not lock clients into long-term contracts. Every engagement is fixed-fee, with clearly defined deliverables and timelines.
