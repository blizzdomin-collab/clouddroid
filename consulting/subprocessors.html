Subprocessors

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Regulatory disclosure: PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) or any other UK or EU financial regulator. The subprocessors listed below support our FinOps and IT cost optimisation consulting services only. Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. is included as our payment processor under standard e-commerce arrangements; we do not offer payment, e-money, banking, or any other regulated financial service.

This page lists the third-party subprocessors authorised to process personal data on behalf of PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD in connection with the operation of consulting.clouddroid.eu and the delivery of our consulting services. We engage only subprocessors that provide adequate technical and organisational data protection measures.

We will provide at least 30 days' notice by updating this page and, where appropriate, by emailing active clients before engaging any new subprocessor. Clients may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds; in such cases we will work in good faith to find an acceptable resolution.

Payment Processing Disclosure

All card payments on consulting.clouddroid.eu are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. ("Stripe"), an authorised payment institution regulated by the Central Bank of Ireland and operating across the EEA under its Stripe Europe Services Agreement.

PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD does not store, process, or have access to your full card number, CVV/CVC, or other sensitive authentication data. Card data is collected and held exclusively by Stripe in accordance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Level 1.
