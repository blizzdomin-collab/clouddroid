Why Clouddroid

Clouddroid is the consulting brand of PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD, a UK-registered private limited company (Company Number 16993940) specialising in cloud financial management and IT cost optimisation. Our team brings deep expertise in FinOps practices, vendor negotiations, and technology budgeting to help enterprise organisations make informed financial decisions about their cloud infrastructure.

We operate as an independent advisory firm. All engagements are conducted under the direct contractual relationship between the client and PRIME CONSULTING GROUP LTD, with clear fixed-fee pricing and defined deliverables. Our work is governed by English law and we maintain strict client confidentiality at all times.
